🌕 old_moon



Welcome to old_moon, a raw network packet sniffer that digs through your local interface's dirty laundry so you don't have to. Whether you are debugging a broken protocol, figuring out why your smart fridge is sending 4GB of data to an unknown IP at 3 AM, or just trying to look terrifyingly competent in front of non-tech friends, old_moon is here to illuminate the digital noise.
